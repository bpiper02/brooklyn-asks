LEGAL / PRIVACY

Use the record. Check the source.

Brooklyn Asks is an independent, unofficial reading and research aid. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the City of New York, any Community Board, or any City agency.

01

Authoritative sources

NYC Open Data and the originating City agencies remain the authoritative sources for City records. Brooklyn Asks may normalize, classify, summarize, join, or reorganize public records to make them easier to inspect. Always open the cited source when accuracy is material.

02

No warranty

The project is provided for informational and research purposes, without warranties of completeness, accuracy, timeliness, availability, or fitness for a particular purpose. Public records can be corrected, overwritten, refreshed, or removed by their publishers.

03

Derived labels are not City findings

Labels such as “supported,” “funding uncertain,” “recurring theme,” or “repeated project” are reading aids derived from source text or matching logic. They are not official City determinations unless the underlying source itself says so.

04

Response ≠ delivery

An agency response, funding statement, or recommendation is not proof that a project was physically built, completed, or delivered. Brooklyn Asks keeps published response and verified real-world outcome conceptually separate.

05

Privacy

The current site does not require accounts and does not intentionally collect personal information through its own forms. External services reached through links—such as GitHub, NYC Open Data, or social networks—operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

06

Data and code

City data remains subject to the terms and ownership of its originating agencies. NYC Open Data permits public reuse, while the City retains ownership of agency data. Brooklyn Asks’ original application code is separately licensed in the repository.

07

Not professional advice

Nothing on this site is legal, financial, engineering, procurement, planning, or other professional advice. Do not rely on the site as the sole basis for a consequential decision.

08

Corrections

If a record, label, source, or interpretation appears wrong, open a public issue so the correction can be reviewed and documented.
