COVERAGE NOTE

Historical does not mean complete.

01

Official documents

Historical records are transcribed from official Community District Needs statements and budget-request documents, with source URLs retained.

02

No invented response

If the historical source does not include an agency response, Brooklyn Asks says so rather than inferring one.

03

Coverage grows

The goal is to expand board-by-board and year-by-year while keeping provenance visible on every record.
