HOW IT WORKS

What the records can tell you.

01

The neighborhood raised it

Community Board requests show what a district formally asked the city to address.

02

The city answered

An agency response tells you what the city said. It does not automatically prove a project was funded or finished.

03

It came back

A similar issue appearing again is a sign of persistence. It is not automatic proof that nothing happened.

Brooklyn Asks is an unofficial reading tool. Every record links back to the official NYC source.
