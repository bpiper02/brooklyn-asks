HOW TO READ THIS

Useful context without pretending the records say more than they do.

01

The neighborhood raised it

Community Board requests and priorities are formal signals of what a district asked the city to address. They are not a complete measure of every problem residents face.

02

The city responded

An agency saying it supports something does not prove the project was funded, built, or completed. Response and real-world outcome remain separate facts.

03

It came back

“Still coming back” means a similar normalized request appeared for the same board in multiple fiscal years. It is a persistence signal, not automatic proof of government failure.

Unofficial public-interest reading aid. Open the cited NYC source for authoritative wording. Brooklyn Asks makes scattered records easier to remember and compare; it does not replace the underlying record.
